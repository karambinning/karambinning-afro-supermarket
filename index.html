A note from the shop

Why this shop is here.

Walk into a supermarket on Narborough Road and you'll find rice, you'll find oil, you'll find chicken. You won't find ewedu fresh enough to wrap your fingers in, palm oil that's the right red, or someone behind the counter who knows what you're cooking on Sunday.

This is the shop for those things — for the people who grew up cooking, who came here from Lagos or Kingston or Accra, and who don't want to apologise for the ingredients they buy.

If we have it, it's on the shelf. If we don't, ring and ask — we'll bring it in by Friday.
